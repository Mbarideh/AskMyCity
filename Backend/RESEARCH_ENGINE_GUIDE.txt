AskMyCity Research Engine (v0.6.3)
==================================

What changed
------------
- Research runs in waves instead of stopping after the first three businesses.
- Each wave researches up to AI_RESEARCH_BATCH_SIZE businesses in parallel.
- It stops after AI_RESEARCH_TARGET_RESULTS verified matches are found or after
  AI_RESEARCH_MAX_CANDIDATES candidates have been checked.
- Positive AND negative findings are cached by business + cuisine + dish + price.
- Repeating the same search reuses the cache and avoids paying for the same web
  research again.
- AI profile facts are merged, not blindly overwritten, so prior menu knowledge
  remains available for later searches.

Recommended starting values
---------------------------
AI_RESEARCH_BATCH_SIZE=3
AI_RESEARCH_MAX_CANDIDATES=12
AI_RESEARCH_TARGET_RESULTS=3

The first difficult search can still take several minutes. Later identical
searches should be much faster because completed research is cached.
